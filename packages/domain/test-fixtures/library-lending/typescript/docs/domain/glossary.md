# Glossary

## Loan

A member borrows one book for a fixed period.

- **Loan** (context: lending): one copy lent to a member

## Member

A person with a library card who may hold loans.

- **Member**: a registered patron of the library

## Account

Lending and Billing both speak of an account; the glossary entry is Billing's.

- **Account** (context: billing): the payer who settles invoices

## Fine

A charge for a book returned after its due date.

## Invoice

A bill that Billing raises for a member's unpaid fines.
